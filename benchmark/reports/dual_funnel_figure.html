Fig. 1. (Why this figure) We compare a RapidChiplet-style independent-evaluation pipeline against our joint dual-funnel screen on a 12-case, real-parameter-only grid (2 topologies × 2 sizes × 3 industrial IO-parameter sets; toy calibration parameters deliberately excluded). (What it shows) The independent pipeline accepts 10 / 12 configurations by evaluating throughput, split-class power, and thermal in separate channels, with thermal treated as a reporting metric rather than a binding gate. Our joint screen first enforces package-level physical coupling (Funnel 1) and then the worst-load routing envelope over the full permutation conjugacy classes (Funnel 2). (What it means) 8 / 12 configurations escape the independent check but are rejected by at least one binding constraint in the coupled formulation — a 66.7 % additional infeasibility-detection rate on this grid. These correspond to designs that would be passed to downstream placement and routing, only to fail μbump, thermal, or congestion checks there.
